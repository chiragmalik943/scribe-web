Privacy

Your recordings stay
on your device.

Scribe is a local app first. Nothing is uploaded unless you switch it on, and every switch is one page deep.

  • On-device transcription

    Dictation runs locally on the Parakeet model. It works with no internet at all, and nothing is uploaded to transcribe it.

  • Nothing leaves by default

    Cloud transcription and assistant replies are two separate switches. Both start off, and the app shows you which are live.

  • Your data, deletable

    Recordings, transcripts and tasks live in one folder on your device that you can open, export, or erase in a single action.

  • No training on your calls

    Your recordings are never used to train models — ours or anyone else’s. That is a contractual term, not a setting.
